About
AImage is a premium, tailor-made AI Solution for the photographers to prove to the market that they are also adapting to cutting edge technologies like AI to satisfy their customers. It helps the Participants, Guests & Invitees to receive all their photos captured at the event, which was nearly impossible before. 
![Share occasion photos to the partners easily](https://www.kratosolutions.com/images/Event-photo-sharing-app.png)
![Now sending event photos to people is easy - Just a click away ](https://www.kratosolutions.com/images/Aimage-Photo-sharing-solutions--for-any-event.png)
01
As usual, Photographers can stay busy capturing all the important moments of the event and attendees can placidly focus on posing for any number of snaps. 
![event photo sharing to anyone who attended is handy now](https://www.kratosolutions.com/images/Share-the-event-memories-with-your-loved-ones--Aimage.png)
02
Photographers shall merely share the photo album of the event to AImage via Google Drive. 
![Aimage Photo sharing solutions - for any event](https://www.kratosolutions.com/images/Share-any-event-photos-to-the-attendees-easily.png)
![Share the event memories with your loved ones - Aimage](https://www.kratosolutions.com/images/Share-occasion-photos-to-the-partners-easily.png)
03
Attendees those who want their photos back, can just scan the QR Code from the banner, provide their contact details and upload a live Selfie Photo. 
![Aimage event photo sharing - Artificial Intelligence](https://www.kratosolutions.com/images/Aimage-event-photo-sharing--Artificial-Intelligence.png)
04.AI Engine
AImage uses advanced AI algorithm to Identify the photos of the individuals who have uploaded their Selfie using QR and Isolates them from the complete photo album. 
05
AImage surprises all the attendees by sharing their photos captured during the event to their Whatsapp directly.
