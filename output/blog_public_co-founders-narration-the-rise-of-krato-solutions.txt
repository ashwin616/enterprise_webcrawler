# Blog
#### Co-founders Narration : "The Rise of Krato Solutions"
By: **Kamakshi Gopalan and Sarathkumar Kannan** - Aug 07, 2019 
![](https://www.kratosolutions.com/blog/public/fileman/Uploads/1n.png)
This blog aims at formally introducing ourselves to the world through written communication.
We have two Co-founders of Krato Solutions, Kamakshi and Sarathkumar, waving a big hi to all of you. They are here to introduce their first venture, narrate its experiences and plans. 
Let’s observe the dialog flow between them to gain clarity on Krato Solutions.
_At Loyola College on Aug1, 2017:_
**Sarathkumar:** Hey, buddies! Currently, we can hear only a few words buzzing when we look at any industry, ”Machine learning”, “Artificial Intelligence” and “Data revolution”. We being from the same field, why don’t we use it to our fullest potential and start a Data Analytics company? I feel we have the skill sets, passion and dedication towards what we are learning, and I think we can make it big.
**Kamakshi:** That’s an interesting idea! However, we should go with a proper plan to succeed in this. First, let’s keep our eyes and ears open so we learn all the advanced and emerging solutions; then we shall move on the road ahead.
_Mar, 2018:_
**Kamakshi:** I think the learning process is going well. Still, I feel there should be some corporate experience to have a better perspective for our venture.
**Sarathkumar:** I agree with this. Let’s join different companies and get back a year later with diverse knowledge and experience.
_May, 2018 – May, 2019:_
They started working, learning process continued and they also started building a base for their company by expanding contacts, generating ideas, preparing case studies and POCs. And the day came for them to turn brave… 
_June 19, 2019:_
They started working full time for Krato Solutions. They rejoiced their decision and were raring to go.
**Sarathkumar:** Let’s pitch our contacts for Analytics services with our solutions.
While fishing for eggs, they found a chicken in Blueoshan, which could incubate them. They discovered a mentor in A. Shanthakumar who had the Midas’s touch to turn the ordinary eggs into a golden one. 
**Both:** Now the platform is ready for us to roar, to our fullest. Our campaign starts right here from you.
**Special note:**
We thank God Almighty for showering His choicest blessings on us, Kumar for being a supreme mentor, Clients and Customers for their contribution in our growth, our family for their indelible support, our friends for their constant encouragement and our team of 5 for holding together through any crisis. Thank you for believing in us!
